# A simple daily LifeOS workflow

LifeOS works best when the daily check-in stays lightweight.

## Morning — choose direction

1. Open Overview.
2. Add the 1–3 tasks that matter most today.
3. Pick the habit you most want to protect.
4. Look at one active goal and choose a small next action.

## During the day — record, don't over-plan

- Mark tasks complete when finished.
- Check off habits after doing them.
- Use Focus for a short, distraction-limited work session.
- Capture ideas in Notes instead of interrupting the current task.

## Evening — reflect

1. Use Journal for a short reflection.
2. Check tomorrow's calendar.
3. Review progress without treating a missed day as failure.

## Keep it sustainable

The dashboard is a guide, not a score of your worth. A small repeatable action is better than building an elaborate routine you cannot maintain.
