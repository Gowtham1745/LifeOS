# Finance section guide

LifeOS can be used as a lightweight manual snapshot of income and expenses.

## Suggested use

1. Record a clear transaction label.
2. Enter the amount and choose income or expense.
3. Use a consistent category.
4. Review totals to understand the entries you have recorded.

## Important limitations

The current finance section is not connected to bank accounts and does not verify transactions. Totals only reflect what you enter. It is not a tax, accounting, or investment service.

Do not store bank passwords, card numbers, account numbers, or other financial credentials. Because data is stored locally in the browser, export a backup before clearing site data.
