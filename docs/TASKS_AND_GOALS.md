# Tasks and goals

## Tasks

Use tasks for actions that can be completed. Write titles as clear verbs where possible, for example, “Send the project update” instead of “Project update”. Choose a priority that helps you decide what to do next, not what to worry about.

Mark a task complete when it is done. Delete entries that no longer matter rather than letting the list become an archive of guilt.

## Goals

Use goals for outcomes that take more than one session. Give each goal a clear title, a realistic deadline, and a progress estimate. Update progress when you have evidence of movement.

## A practical rhythm

- Daily: select a few tasks.
- Weekly: review goals and choose the next milestone.
- Monthly: remove goals that no longer match your priorities.

Progress indicators are estimates to support reflection; they are not automatic measures of achievement.
