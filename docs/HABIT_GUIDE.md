# Habit tracker guide

A useful habit is specific enough that you can tell whether you did it.

## Make habits easy to repeat

- Start with a small version you can do on a difficult day.
- Use an action and a cue, such as “Read one page after breakfast”.
- Track completion honestly.
- Review what made the habit easier or harder once a week.

## Understanding streaks

A streak counts consecutive calendar days represented in the app's completion dates. Time zone and the device's current date affect which day is considered today. A streak is feedback, not a reason to continue an unhealthy routine or punish yourself for missing a day.

## Keep the list focused

Three habits you actually practice are more useful than twenty habits you rarely check.
